# Bundled assets

Demo photos downloaded from Unsplash image endpoints and bundled for offline presentation:

- Kyoto street / Japan: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1493976040374-85c8e12f0c0e
- Workspace / business: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1497366754035-f200968a6e72
- Forest / calmer life: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1441974231531-c6227db76b6e

These are inspiration images, not representations of the user's actual memories. Real photos are selected by the user. Photo usage is subject to the [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license).

Fonts are from the Google Fonts source repository:

- [Manrope](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope) — SIL Open Font License; included as `assets/fonts/Manrope-LICENSE.txt`.
- [Cormorant Garamond](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond) — SIL Open Font License; included as `assets/fonts/Cormorant-LICENSE.txt`.

Launcher artwork is an original geometric circle-and-sprout mark created for this project. Flutter native runner templates come from the Flutter 3.47.5 SDK; Flutter's license is included as `FLUTTER-LICENSE.txt`. Gradle wrapper files originate from the Gradle 9.3.1 source repository; its license is included as `GRADLE-LICENSE.txt`.
